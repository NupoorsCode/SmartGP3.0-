Orlistat (Xenical)

A capsule taken with meals that reduces the amount of fat absorbed from food. A long-established option with a different exclusion profile to the GLP-1 treatments.

Prescription-only medicine

Orlistat (Xenical) can only be prescribed after a video appointment with one of our clinicians, who must independently confirm your height and weight. We cannot prescribe it from your answers alone.

Is it suitable for me?

  • Adults with a BMI of 30 or above, or 28 and above with a weight-related condition
  • People who cannot take, or would rather not take, a GLP-1 treatment

How do I use it?

  • One capsule with each main meal containing fat, up to three times a day
  • Skip the dose if a meal contains no fat

How does it work?

  • Blocks an enzyme that breaks down dietary fat, so roughly a third of the fat you eat passes through undigested.

Strengths and prices

Your clinician decides which strength you start on — almost always the lowest — and whether to increase it later. You are never charged for a strength you have not been prescribed.
