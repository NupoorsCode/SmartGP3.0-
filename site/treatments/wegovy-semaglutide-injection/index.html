Wegovy (semaglutide injection)

A once-weekly injection licensed for weight management alongside diet and exercise, titrated over about 16 weeks to a maintenance dose.

Prescription-only medicine

Wegovy (semaglutide injection) can only be prescribed after a video appointment with one of our clinicians, who must independently confirm your height and weight. We cannot prescribe it from your answers alone.

Is it suitable for me?

  • Adults with a BMI of 30 or above
  • Or a BMI of 27 to 29.9 with a weight-related health condition
  • Not suitable if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy

How do I use it?

  • One injection each week on the same day
  • Rotate the injection site between the stomach, thigh and upper arm
  • Keep refrigerated until first use

How does it work?

  • Semaglutide mimics a gut hormone that signals fullness to the brain and slows stomach emptying.

Strengths and prices

Your clinician decides which strength you start on — almost always the lowest — and whether to increase it later. You are never charged for a strength you have not been prescribed.
