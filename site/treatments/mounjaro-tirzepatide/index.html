Mounjaro (tirzepatide)

A once-weekly injection licensed for weight management alongside diet and exercise. The dose is increased in steps over several months under clinical supervision.

Prescription-only medicine

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) can only be prescribed after a video appointment with one of our clinicians, who must independently confirm your height and weight. We cannot prescribe it from your answers alone.

Is it suitable for me?

  • Adults with a BMI of 30 or above
  • Or a BMI of 27 to 29.9 with a weight-related health condition
  • Not suitable if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy
  • A clinician makes the final decision with you on the video call

How do I use it?

  • One injection each week, on the same day, into the stomach, thigh or upper arm
  • A new pen is used for each dose
  • Store in the fridge between 2°C and 8°C until first use

How does it work?

  • Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors that regulate appetite and how full you feel after eating.
  • It is used alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity, not instead of them.

Strengths and prices

Your clinician decides which strength you start on — almost always the lowest — and whether to increase it later. You are never charged for a strength you have not been prescribed.

Other important information

  • Common side effects include nausea, diarrhoea, constipation and injection-site reactions, most often in the first weeks after a dose increase.
  • Report any suspected side effect through the MHRA Yellow Card scheme.
