Consultation

Advice-only consultation

A 20-minute video appointment with a clinician to discuss your options, your history and whether medical weight management is right for you. No prescription is issued from this appointment.

Prescription-only medicine

Advice-only consultation can only be prescribed after a video appointment with one of our clinicians, who must independently confirm your height and weight. We cannot prescribe it from your answers alone.

Is it suitable for me?

  • You are considering treatment but want to ask questions first
  • You have been declined elsewhere and want to understand why
  • You want a second opinion on a treatment you are already using

How do I use it?

  • Book a slot at a time that suits you
  • Join the video call at your appointment time
  • Receive a written summary of what was discussed

How does it work?

  • This is a conversation, not an assessment for supply. If you decide to go ahead afterwards you complete the full questionnaire and book a treatment consultation.

Strengths and prices

Your clinician decides which strength you start on — almost always the lowest — and whether to increase it later. You are never charged for a strength you have not been prescribed.
